The Problem

Why Privileged Access Programmes Fail

Identity security programmes stall for three consistent, structural reasons. Recognising them is the first step — but only verified discovery resolves them.

Incomplete Asset Registers

Privileged identity programmes cannot succeed without a verified, comprehensive register of privileged accounts, service accounts, and administrative roles across both Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID. Most organisations discover they are working from an assumption, not a fact.

Governance That Ages Out

Identity governance frameworks deteriorate from the moment they are written. Without continuous validation mechanisms and defined ownership, policy documents become a record of intent rather than a description of reality. Audit cycles reveal the gap — often at the worst possible moment.

Remediation Without Scope

Remediation workstreams launched without a verified baseline scope are expensive, disruptive, and frequently incomplete. Effort is applied to visible problems while structural weaknesses remain unaddressed. The result is a programme that appears to progress but does not resolve the underlying risk.

The Methodology

The ODA 10+7 Framework

A four-volume methodology reference set aligned with Microsoft RAMP and the Microsoft Enterprise Access Model. Structured. Sequenced. Auditable.

Volume I
01

Tier 0 Discovery

A structured, systematic discovery of all Tier 0 assets across the hybrid estate. Produces a verified, comprehensive register of privileged accounts, high-value targets, and control plane assets in both Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID. Nothing is assumed. Everything is confirmed.

Volume II
02

Governance Framework

Assessment of existing governance structures against defined, measurable criteria. Identifies governance gaps, expired or absent ownership models, and policy-versus-reality divergence. Produces an actionable gap analysis with prioritised findings and remediation guidance.

Volume III
03

Enterprise Access Model Design Pattern

Architectural design patterns for implementing the Microsoft Enterprise Access Model in hybrid estates. Translates the conceptual framework into environment-specific, deliverable architecture — including tiering models, delegation design, and privilege segmentation across on-premises and cloud control planes.

Volume IV
04

RAMP Remediation Playbook

A structured remediation playbook aligned with the Microsoft Rapid Modernisation Plan (RAMP). Sequences remediation activities by risk priority, defines success criteria, and provides implementation guidance. Built for environments where remediation must be sequenced carefully to avoid operational disruption.
